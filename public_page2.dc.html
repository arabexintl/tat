About Us

Arabex International is an international translation house based in Kerala, India. We translate from Arabic into any language and from any language into Arabic — certificates, legal, academic, Islamic, CV and media content — with precision, care and confidentiality.

Beyond translation, we guide students toward Arab universities and scholarships, prepare professionals for the Gulf job market, and build platforms that carry Arabic language, learning and news to a wider audience.

Whatever the document or destination, Arabex helps you communicate clearly, confidently, and without borders.
